# Code of Conduct

SFDX Talk is a place for Salesforce developers to learn from each other. We want it to be welcoming for everyone, regardless of experience level, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be respectful and constructive, in pull requests, issues, discussions and article comments.
- Critique content and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and help newcomers.
- Credit others' work and respect licenses.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the discussion, or SEO link-dropping.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Posting confidential customer or employer data, credentials or org details.
- Deliberately misleading technical content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, pull requests and discussions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously do so.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or post, or contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (it is private and reaches maintainers only). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
